# Runtime 05 · vrc-two-gates

**Status: 🟢 standing watch (14/14).**

The credential ceremony as a dream-agent cycle — the direct descendant of
`dtgwg-zkp-tf-mage/runtimes/07-trust-graph-formation`. There the fold minted
trust-graph edges on personhood + mutual consent; here it proves the Gatehouse
VRC at the gate on the TWO GATES (CLAUDE.md rule 5): a supervisor approval AND
a passed understanding challenge, bilaterally signed, every evidence hash
resolving into a ledger that itself verifies (runtime 03's law, taken by
import). The TSP framing is carried in the proof roles: the ceremony is a
relationship formation between two VIDs, `tsp:relationship:issuer` and
`tsp:relationship:subject`, and both must sign — the spanning made mechanical.

The **Mage** proposes the smallest well-formed credential for an assessment;
the **Swordsman** proves the ceremony's rules from the candidate's own parts +
the verified ledger, in order: ledger verifies → collapsed sovereignty blocks
before any gate is weighed (det(Σ) ≤ 0 or non-PSD ⇒ BLOCKED ⇒ hold ⇒ **no
credential at all**) → closed verdict lexicon (VALIDATED→fly, MIRAGE→sandbox,
BLOCKED→hold; never a third vocabulary) → both gates, distinct kinds →
evidence anchored → exactly two proofs, two roles, two distinct parties →
tier IS the stratum.

Reject reasons: `ledger-does-not-verify`, `collapsed-pair-must-block`,
`blocked-no-issuance`, `unknown-verdict`, `decision-lexicon-drift`,
`missing-supervisor-gate`, `missing-understanding-gate`,
`evidence-not-in-ledger`, `not-bilateral`, `roles-not-spanning`,
`self-signed-not-bilateral`, `tier-stratum-mismatch`.

Properties: G1 the canary flies at tier 6 · G2 either gate alone is refused
(rule 5, stated twice on purpose) · G3 unforgeable alone — single proof and
self-signed both refused; the frozen schema pins the same rule
(minItems=maxItems=2) · G4 forged evidence and a broken ledger void the
ceremony · G5 the lexicon is closed (invented verdict, mirage-claiming-flight
both refused) · G6 collapsed det holds deployment even with both gates passed ·
G7 a flattering tier refused.

already live and this runtime mirrors it). When WP2 lands issuance, this
