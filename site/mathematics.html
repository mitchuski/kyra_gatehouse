The mathematics

Companion-guide depth. Every formula below runs in the engine and is recomputed by independent verification lanes; nothing here is decorative.

The sovereignty matrix and its volume

An assessment scores six pairwise separations σ ∈ [0,1] between the four forces (protect, project, reflect, connect). The 4×4 matrix Σ has unit diagonal and carries the residual correlation off-diagonal:

Σij = 1 − σij (i ≠ j) · Σii = 1

Full separation everywhere (σ = 1) gives the identity matrix — det(Σ) = 1, the full tetrahedron. No separation (σ = 0) gives the all-ones matrix — det(Σ) = 0, total collapse. det(Σ) is the sovereignty volume, and gating is multiplicative: one collapsed pair takes total value to zero. Positive semi-definiteness is checked over all principal minors (Sylvester's criterion alone certifies only the definite case).

det ≤ 0 → hold · 0 < det < θfly → sandbox · det ≥ θfly → fly

The 64-vertex lattice, and tier

Each separation, thresholded, sets one bit of a 6-bit word (weights 32/16/8/4/2/1 = Protection/Delegation/Memory/Connection/Computation/Value). The word is a vertex of the Boolean lattice {0,1}⁶ — 64 positions in seven strata by popcount (1·6·15·20·15·6·1) — and tier IS the stratum: rank is located, not assigned.

Relationship value and integrity

A(τ) = α · ln(1 + |τ|) · h(τ)

|τ| is the count of audited interactions; h(τ) is the integrity fraction — the share of the history whose hash chain verifies. Unverified history contributes nothing: h(τ) multiplies, so a broken chain voids accumulated standing. The audit ledger is content-addressed (canonical bytes: recursive sorted keys, no whitespace, sha256), chained from a zero genesis, and re-derivable by any auditor in any language.

The witness draw (anti-grooming)

indexi = sha256(canonHash ‖ uint32BE(i)) mod remaining

Deep-verification probes are sampled without replacement, seeded by the sha256 of the agent's own canonicalised submission. The draw is deterministic to an auditor and unpredictable to the agent at writing time — the assessed party cannot rehearse its own audit.

The reconstruction ceiling (Act II — in build)

R < 1

The pooling claim: attestation bundles are minimised by construction — counts become thresholds, timestamps become windows, enumerations become cardinalities — so that no coalition of pooled bundles reconstructs any single institution's underlying data. The formal statement and its demonstration land with the two-authority pooling build; its status is tracked openly in Evidence.
