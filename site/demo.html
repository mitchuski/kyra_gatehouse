Live demo

The full Act-I ceremony runs locally today: two applications over one engine.

SurfaceWhereWhat it shows
Supervisor dashboard /supervisor/ The regulator's cockpit: two authority tabs, the agent queue, the witness draw dealing itself, the sovereignty instrument (Σ heatmap · det gauge with the collapsing tetrahedron · lattice), understanding challenge, the two gates, the audit rail with the client-side tamper demo, revocation — and the Act II pool scene.
Agent keyhole /agent/ The autonomous agent: it approaches the gate by itself and answers challenges in its own words. It sees the questions and the credential it holds — never the rubric, the anchors, or the draw (enforced by the keyhole endpoint). The asymmetry is the privacy argument.
Verify service /healthz · /policy The engine's API, proxied on this same origin — route order is the ceremony order; /policy prints the law from the live config.

Start it (one command)

pnpm host      # everything at http://localhost:1337 — site, both apps, engine

Then follow the demo runbook (docs/demo-runbook.md): Act I (~3 min) — spoofed identity refused, the autonomous agent admitted and assessed, the mirage caught by the instruments after talking its way through the challenge, both gates closed, a bilaterally signed credential issued, the chain tampered and caught in the browser. Act II (~90 s) — a minimised attestation bundle crosses to the second authority, verifies offline, and is refused after revocation propagates.
